🛰️ Satellite Art Projects

Orbital Sunrise (1965)

Artist: Alexei Leonov (Soviet cosmonaut and artist)
Location: Orbit
Type: Drawing / Space Art
Technical Support: Soviet Space Program
Site: In orbit, during Voskhod 2 mission

“Orbital Sunrise” was the first artwork created in outer space. Alexei Leonov, while outside the Voskhod 2 spacecraft, used colored pencils on paper to sketch the view of Earth’s horizon illuminated by the rising Sun. The drawing became a symbolic gesture of human artistic presence beyond Earth.

The Moon Museum (1969)

Artists: Forrest Myers, with Andy Warhol, Robert Rauschenberg, John Chamberlain, Claes Oldenburg, David Novros
Location: The Moon (Apollo 12 Lunar Module “Intrepid”)
Type: Micro ceramic art panel
Technical Support: Bell Laboratories (New Jersey, USA)
Site: Lunar surface, hidden on Apollo 12

“The Moon Museum” was a secret art project sent to the Moon in 1969. The tiny ceramic chip carried six engraved drawings from leading contemporary artists, smuggled aboard Apollo 12. It represents a playful yet profound attempt to insert art into the history of space exploration.
