Satellite Art

Satellite art refers to sustained artistic practices that take place in outer space, including those on orbital satellites, space stations, deep space probes, and the Moon. It specifically includes art satellites, scientific satellites with artistic functions, and artworks on deep space probes; art projects conducted on the International Space Station (ISS), China’s Tiangong Space Station (TSS), and the former Soviet Union’s Mir Space Station (Mir); as well as artworks that have been sent to the Moon.
